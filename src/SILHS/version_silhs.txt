    Clip rcm after l_use_cloud_cover replaces it with rcm_in_layer (#1421)
    
    * Clip rcm after l_use_cloud_cover replaces it with rcm_in_layer
    
    With l_use_cloud_cover=.true., pdf_closure_driver overwrites rcm with
    rcm_in_layer after clip_rcm has already run. At a cloud top or base,
    rcm_in_layer is rcm divided by the vertical cloud fraction, so it can
    exceed rtm. The resulting rvm = rtm - rcm < 0 stops the run in the
    radiation input check. mc3e with flag10 of
    run_bindiff_w_flags_config_core_flags.json failed this way at level 61
    after 3983 steps (rcm_in_layer = 1.17e-5, rtm = 9.09e-6 kg/kg).
    
    Call clip_rcm again after the replacement. mc3e now completes its full
    12960 steps with flag10 and with l_use_cloud_cover=.true. alone.
    
    BIT_CHANGING only when l_use_cloud_cover=.true. and rcm_in_layer > rtm
    at some level; then rcm is clipped to just below rtm. Default runs are unaffected.
